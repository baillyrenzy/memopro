How to Read the Supplement Facts Panel

The Supplement Facts panel is the most reliable source of information about any capsule. It lists serving size, servings per container, each ingredient and its amount, plus allergen statements and "other ingredients" such as capsule material and fillers. When you receive your bottle, check these points:

  • Serving size: how many capsules make one serving, since this decides how long a 60-capsule bottle lasts.
  • Amount per ingredient: look for specific milligram amounts rather than vague blends.
  • Other ingredients: check for allergens such as soy, gluten, dairy or shellfish if you have sensitivities.
  • Lot number and expiry: useful if you ever need to contact support.

Getting the Most From a Cognitive Support Supplement

No capsule works in isolation. Brain health is shaped by daily habits, and a supplement is best seen as one small piece of a bigger routine. These habits are widely recommended for supporting focus and memory:

😴 Quality Sleep

Most adults need 7–9 hours. Sleep is when the brain consolidates memories.

🏃 Regular Movement

Walking, cycling or any regular activity supports blood flow to the brain.

🥗 Balanced Diet

Berries, leafy greens, nuts, fish and whole grains are common brain-friendly foods.

🧘 Stress Management

Meditation, breaks and hobbies can help reduce mental fatigue.

📚 Mental Challenge

Reading, puzzles and learning new skills keep the mind active.

💧 Hydration

Even mild dehydration can affect concentration and mood.

A Simple Daily Routine Idea

  • Morning: take your supplement as directed on the label, preferably with food, then plan your three top priorities for the day.
  • Midday: take a short walk and drink water to avoid the afternoon slump.
  • Evening: limit screens before bed and keep a consistent sleep schedule.
  • Weekly: note any changes in focus, mood and memory in a short journal.

Interactions and Medical Conditions

Herbal ingredients can interact with prescription and over-the-counter medicines. Ginkgo in particular is often discussed in relation to blood-thinning medicines and procedures where bleeding risk matters. Speak with a doctor or pharmacist before use if you take any regular medicine, have a bleeding disorder, have a seizure disorder, or have surgery planned.

Storage and Handling

  • Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
  • Keep the cap tightly closed and the bottle out of reach of children.
  • Do not use if the safety seal is broken or the product is past its expiry date.

When to See a Doctor

Supplements cannot diagnose or treat memory problems. If you or a loved one notice persistent forgetfulness, confusion, difficulty with familiar tasks or sudden changes in thinking, please see a healthcare professional promptly rather than relying on a supplement.

How to Judge Results Fairly

  • Write down your baseline focus, mood and sleep before starting.
  • Change only one thing at a time, so you know what is making a difference.
  • Review your notes after 8–12 weeks of consistent use.
  • Stop and seek advice if you notice side effects.
